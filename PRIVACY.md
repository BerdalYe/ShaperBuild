# Privacy

ShaperBuild does not collect personal data unless you make an account.
There is no tracking, no analytics and no advertising, and you do not need
an account to use the app.

## Who is responsible

**Mathias Verdal** publishes ShaperBuild and is responsible for the personal
data of ShaperBuild accounts (the data controller under the GDPR).

Contact: **berdalproductions@gmail.com**

## What is stored, and where

- **On your computer:** designs, settings, saved versions, thumbnails and
  crash-recovery copies stay in the folders you choose and in the app's own
  data folder. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you save a design to the
  cloud.
- **Account and cloud designs (only if you sign in):**
  - your email address, and your password in hashed (unreadable) form;
    ShaperBuild itself never stores your password;
  - the designs you choose to save to the cloud: the design file, its name,
    a small picture of it and when it was changed.

  Only you can see your cloud designs. Your sign-in is kept encrypted on
  your computer. With *Continue with Google*, Google tells the account
  service your email address.

**Why:** to give you the account and keep your cloud designs, which you ask
for by signing up (GDPR article 6(1)(b), performing a contract with you).

**How long:** until you delete them. *Delete account* (in the account menu
on the home screen) deletes your account and every cloud design straight
away. Designs saved on your computer stay.

## Services that handle the data

- **Supabase** (Supabase Inc.) runs the accounts, the database and the
  file storage for cloud designs.
- **Brevo** (Sendinblue SAS, France) sends the account emails, such as the
  code for choosing a new password. It receives your email address and the
  email's contents.

Both process the data only on ShaperBuild's behalf, under their data
processing agreements. Where data is handled outside the EEA, those
agreements use the EU's standard contractual clauses.

## Other connections

- **Update check:** when it starts, and every few hours while it runs,
  ShaperBuild asks GitHub (github.com, run by GitHub, Inc.) whether a newer
  version has been published. Like any web request, this lets GitHub see
  your computer's IP address. No design data or personal information is
  sent. You can turn this off in Settings (*Check for updates
  automatically and install them on restart*).
- **AI assistant (only if you set it up):** what you type in the AI chat,
  a description of your design (its bodies, sketches and the results of each
  step) and, with Claude, pictures of the design are sent to the AI service
  you chose: Anthropic for Claude, or the address you entered for another
  service (nothing leaves your computer if that is Ollama or LM Studio on
  it). You choose and pay for that service, and its own privacy terms apply.
  Your API key is stored encrypted on your computer and only sent to that
  service.
- **AI apps over MCP (off unless you turn it on):** ShaperBuild then answers
  apps on this computer that have the secret token (such as Claude Desktop).
  Those apps may send what they get to their own AI service.
- Links in the app (for example to the source code) open in your own web
  browser.

## Your rights

You can see, correct, download or delete your data, and object to how it is
used. Most of this you can do in the app: your cloud designs are listed on
the home screen, where you can open, save a copy of, rename or delete them,
and *Delete account* removes everything. For anything else, email
**berdalproductions@gmail.com** and you will get an answer within a month.

If you think your data is handled wrongly, you can complain to the Norwegian
Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.no) or the authority
where you live.

## Changes

If this notice changes, the new version is published here and with the next
version of the app.
