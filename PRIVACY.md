# Privacy

ShaperBuild does not collect personal data unless you make an account.

- There is no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. You do not need an
  account to use ShaperBuild.
- Designs, settings, saved versions, thumbnails and crash-recovery copies
  are stored on your computer (in the folders you choose and in the app's
  own data folder), unless you save a design to the cloud.
- **Account and cloud designs (only if you sign in):** your email address,
  your password (stored by the account service in hashed form, never by
  ShaperBuild) and the designs you choose to save to the cloud (the design
  file, its name, a small picture of it and when it was changed) are stored
  with Supabase, the service that runs ShaperBuild accounts, on behalf of
  whoever publishes this copy of ShaperBuild. With *Continue with Google*,
  Google tells the account service your email address. Only you can see
  your cloud designs. Your sign-in is kept encrypted on your computer.
  *Delete account* (in the account menu on the home screen) deletes your
  account and every cloud design straight away; designs saved on your
  computer stay.
- **Update check:** when it starts, and every few hours while it runs,
  ShaperBuild asks GitHub (github.com, run by GitHub, Inc.) whether a newer
  version has been published. Like any web request, this lets GitHub see
  your computer's IP address. No design data or personal information is
  sent. You can turn this off in Settings (*Check for updates
  automatically and install them on restart*); see GitHub's privacy statement for what GitHub does with
  the request.
- **AI assistant (only if you set it up):** what you type in the AI chat,
  a description of your design (its bodies, sketches and the results of each
  step) and, with Claude, pictures of the design are sent to the AI service
  you chose: Anthropic for Claude, or the address you entered for another
  service (nothing leaves your computer if that is Ollama or LM Studio on
  it). That service's own privacy terms apply. Your API key is stored
  encrypted on your computer and only sent to that service.
- **AI apps over MCP (off unless you turn it on):** ShaperBuild then answers
  apps on this computer that have the secret token (such as Claude Desktop).
  Those apps may send what they get to their own AI service.
- Links in the app (for example to the source code) open in your own web
  browser.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
